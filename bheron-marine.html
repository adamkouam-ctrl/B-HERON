Our Philosophy

The sea demands

Salt, UV, algae, and mineral deposits inflict constant assault on your vessel's surfaces. BHERON Marine exists to counter this — deploying professional marine-grade compounds, certified teak treatments, and hull polishing systems used on the world's finest yachts.

We operate at leading marinas across Northern Italy and Switzerland, with a fully mobile team that comes to your berth. No slippage required for most services. No disruption to your schedule.
