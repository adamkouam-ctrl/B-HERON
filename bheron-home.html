Our Philosophy

A home that
reflects your standards.

BHERON Home operates on the same principles as our automotive division — methodical, perfectionist, and completely discreet. Our household teams are vetted, uniformed, and trained in the care of exceptional materials: Calacatta marble, aged parquet, silk drapes, and handmade ceramics.

We serve private residences, villas, chalets, and high-specification apartments across Milan, Rome, Florence, Geneva, and Zürich. Every visit is supervised by a senior team leader. Nothing is rushed. Nothing is overlooked.

"Our teams carry only professional-grade, residue-free products — pH-neutral on natural stone, solvent-free on antique furniture, and certifiably non-toxic throughout."
